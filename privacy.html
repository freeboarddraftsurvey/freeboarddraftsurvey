Privacy Policy

Freeboard – Draft Survey · Last updated: 11 October 2026

Freeboard is made by Captain Apio Ramil. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What the app stores

Vessel data, surveys, ballast and trimming figures, reports, company logo, signatures and settings that you enter are saved only on your own device. Freeboard has no user accounts and does not send this data to us or to anyone else.

Files you choose to share

When you export, share or back up a file, it goes only where you send it (for example email or a messaging app). We never receive a copy.

Phone sensors

The list calculator can read the phone's motion sensor to measure heel. The readings are used on the device only and are not stored or sent.

Internet

The app works offline. It may download a public font and, the first time you import an Excel file, the Excel reader library. Links to our YouTube channel open YouTube. No personal data is sent with these.

Ads, tracking and analytics

Freeboard shows no ads and contains no tracking or analytics.

Children

Freeboard is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove all data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
